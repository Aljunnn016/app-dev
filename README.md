# My Favorite Anime: One Piece

**Genre:** Action / Adventure / Fantasy  
**Author:** Eiichiro Oda  

## Overview
One Piece follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, as he explores the Grand Line in search of the mythical treasure known as "One Piece" in order to become the next King of the Pirates.

## Main Characters
* **Monkey D. Luffy** - Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates
* **Roronoa Zoro** - Swordsman
* **Nami** - Navigator
* **Usopp** - Sniper
* **Sanji** - Cook

## Why I Like It
1. Amazing world-building and deep lore.
2. Emotional backstories and great character growth.
3. Epic battles and funny crew interactions.
